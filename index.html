A morning, mapped

Every step has a clear handoff.

How the report gets ready

Designed around a 7:00 shift start
  1. 016:00 AM

    Collection begins

    Power Automate gathers the scheduled source files from SAP, IBP, Celonis, and Tableau.

  2. 02ABOUT 6:30 AM

    Inputs are staged

    Within about 30 minutes, the files are placed in the target folder for the workbook.

  3. 037:00 AM

    Refresh and review

    At shift start, I refresh the Excel system and check for errors or requested updates.

  4. 04BY 7:30 AM

    Ready to send

    After the dashboard looks right, Power Automate emails the report to the team through Outlook.

  5. 05EVERY 30 MINUTES

    Replies become a digest

    A second flow tracks replies and sends me a concise summary of team context and follow ups.

↻When the team requests an update: I can make the change, refresh, and review before sending. The automated prep is already underway, so a new request does not restart the whole file gathering process.
Before and after

Move routine work out of the way.

Built by exploring

Two flows close the reporting loop.

The morning flow prepares and sends the dashboard. The reply flow checks the team’s responses and returns a recurring summary.

Human review stays in the loop. I refresh the workbook, check warnings and requested changes, then send when the report is ready.
What this makes room for

More attention for the operation, less for the repeat work.

Instead of spending the morning moving files and rebuilding the same report, I can focus on the exceptions, respond to the team, and use the saved attention where the operation needs it.
