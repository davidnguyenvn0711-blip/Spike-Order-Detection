The intended improvement

Move the report closer to the start of the day.

Before the system, collecting files, waiting for extraction, refreshing, and checking errors could take 2.5 to 4 hours from starting work to sending. The workflow is designed to prepare data earlier, so the report can be checked and sent more consistently.
