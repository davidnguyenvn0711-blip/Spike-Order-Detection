The process I explored

Automate preparation. Keep review human.

The workbook determines the risk classification. Email automation prepares and shares the report after a person reviews the results.

Power Automate · morning report

Prepare a consistent team report

The scheduled flow reads workbook tables, formats relevant rows, runs a script, and sends the dashboard after review.

1Scheduled start · begin the morning flow
2Read workbook tables · load prepared data
3Filter and format · prepare report content
4Run script and email · share after human checks
Separate flow · team replies

Keep new feedback visible

A second flow retrieves report replies, summarizes their key points, and tracks what is new.

1Retrieve and filter · find relevant replies
2Summarize · group themes and follow-ups
3Track new items · separate fresh feedback
4Share summary · make updates easier to scan
Tool distinction: Power Automate is shown in the documented morning flow. I also taught myself n8n by building a parallel exploration. This page does not claim an AI model makes the workbook’s risk decisions.
The improvement I was aiming for

Move the report closer to the start of the day.

Before the workflow, sending could run late when extraction, refresh, or error checks took longer. My goal was to prepare data earlier, review it at work, and send at a more consistent time.

Before · estimated elapsed time2.5 to 4 hours

From starting work to sending, including extraction waits, refresh, checks, and report preparation.

Workflow · target send window7:30 to 7:45 a.m.

Collect files around 6:00, refresh around 6:30, then review after the 7:00 start.
